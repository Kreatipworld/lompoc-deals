## The facts (verified before publishing)
- **Napoleon Kaufman**, Lompoc High School class of **1990** — rushed for **5,151 yards and 86 touchdowns**
  in high school, among the top prep running backs in California history.
- Two-time All-American at the University of Washington; **first-round pick in 1995**; **six seasons with
  the Oakland Raiders**.
- Coached Bishop O'Dowd (Oakland) to a state title in 2016, 51-25 over six seasons there.
- **Head coach at Dublin High School since 2024** — and tonight Dublin plays at Huyck Stadium,
  his own alma mater's field.
