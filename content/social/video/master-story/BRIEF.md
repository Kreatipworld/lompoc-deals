## The idea

How people used to find a business in Lompoc — asking, the phone book, a flyer on a pole,
the classifieds — told in film language with objects, not actors. Then the turn: a phone on the
table lights up, and the same town is one place. It ends on the number.

Standing rules baked in: generated footage shows THINGS only — no people, no hands, no faces
(the v8 "generated people" cut was rejected; the Terrones hands shot was rejected). The story
carries on the objects and Arthur's voice. If you want a person in it, it has to be real footage
you shoot (an older relative with the phone book at the kitchen table would be the shot).
