## The idea (why it's not another food post)
A split-flap **departures board** — the airport/train kind that clacks through destinations —
except the destinations are Lompoc kitchens and the departure time is when each one opens.
We have never used this grammar. It is motion-native, it is literally a ranked list (what was
asked for: members first, then the rest), and the data driving it is ours: we are the only
place in town with every kitchen's hours in one database.

**Cover (frame 0)** = the board mid-flip, charcoal + gold. Nothing like our recent food-photo
covers or the purple phone cover, which fixes the repetitive-grid problem.
