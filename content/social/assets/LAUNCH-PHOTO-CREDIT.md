# Launch-card background photography — provenance

Both files are **U.S. Space Force** photographs of Falcon 9 night launches from Space Launch
Complex 4 East at Vandenberg Space Force Base — works of the U.S. federal government, in the
**public domain** (17 U.S.C. § 105). No attribution is legally required and no attribution is
burned into the cards, but the photographer is credited here so the provenance is on the record.

## Why these two

Locals see a launch as a bright plume climbing out of the dark hills to the southwest — that is
what both frames show, from the pad the posts actually name. The launch card alternates between
them week to week so two consecutive launch posts don't render as the same picture.

## Rules for adding another

Only U.S. Government imagery (Space Force / Vandenberg SFB / DoD / NASA) or SpaceX's own
public-domain releases. Nothing under a licence that needs attribution printed on the card,
because the card has nowhere to print it.
