# The Lompoc Morning, first Monday edition (Sep 28 2026)

A new weekly format, `monday-paper`. The owner asked for a beginning-of-the-week recap every Monday at 9:15 AM, with the news plus what's coming up. The concept is a morning newspaper on a café table: masthead, front-page headlines, sticky notes, week grid and ticket stubs. 41 s, Maeve voice, new coffee-shop bed. Cost 4.55 cr.
Photos are used only where they show the subject. The news-desk covers for the Braves and Westvale stories showed other places, so I didn't use them: the Braves story uses the Huyck photo and badge, and Westvale gets a sticky note with no photo.
Sources: /news (Braves 5-0 · housing scenarios · Westvale), the events table, and football_games (Fri Oct 2: Braves at Paso Robles 7 PM; Cabrillo vs Morro Bay 7 PM at Huyck).
