# Lompoc News Update — Sept 9 (cards: news-update-sep9-{ig,tt}.png · NOT posted)

Facts (all from our published stories, Sept 9 2026):
1. Mosby and Ball compete for Lompoc mayor — incumbent Mayor Jim Mosby (62) faces Councilman Jeremy Ball (46) in November; Ball wants a new tone, blighted properties, public-safety staffing, renters' rights; Mosby cites housing and jobs, growth-impact fee cuts.
2. Riverbend Park soccer field expansion — first community workshop by Parks & Recreation on a roughly $7 million plan: turf fields, a new parking lot, a walking path; no restrooms/concessions in the initial design; some residents want existing fields fixed first.
3. True Vine Bible Fellowship Community Day Giveaway — Sept 19, 10 AM–2 PM, 533 Avalon St: 1,000 pairs of shoes (while supplies last), free haircuts, food; free and open to everyone.
