## Facebook

40 years in the making. 🐙

In 1986 the Cabrillo High School Aquarium was a school club in one classroom with a few tanks. Today it is more than 20 live and static displays across three galleries — cold salt water, warm salt water and rainforest — maintained by students in the school's Aquarium Science pathway. It has hosted thousands of visitors and holds a 4.6★ rating from 107 Google reviews ("The high school students do an awesome job of informing visitors about the exhibits.").

Tomorrow night, Wednesday Sep 23, 6–8 PM, they open the doors for the 40th anniversary open house and unveil their newest murals, headlined by the Chumash Kelp Forest mural made with NOAA's Channel Islands and Chumash Heritage National Marine Sanctuaries. Admission is free.
