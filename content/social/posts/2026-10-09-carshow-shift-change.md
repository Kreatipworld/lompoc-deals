# Event promo: Shift Change at Ocean & 7th car show (Fri Oct 9 2026)

The first-ever car show at Lompoc Valley Medical Center, Sat Oct 10, 10 AM–2 PM, 1515 E Ocean Ave (event 226). Free to watch, DJ, food trucks; registrations benefit the Lompoc Hospital District Foundation. Facts: LVMC press page (sent by the owner), Noozhawk, Santa Maria Times.
The owner asked to match the look of LVMC's official artwork ("the look and feel is this", "use this as a promo", "so we get attention from the hospital"). Built: a 15s neon HTML animation (scratchpad/eng/carshow) with a tach revving to 7, their artwork as the hero card, a heartbeat line, and an info stack. Juno VO (3 lines), new rock-and-roll bed. The first two cuts (generated daytime chrome scenes, 25s and 17s) were rejected: repeated scenes, too long, wrong look. Credits ≈ 32 (the generated scenes were unused).
