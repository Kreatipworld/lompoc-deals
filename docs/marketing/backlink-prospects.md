## What we're offering (this is the whole pitch)

We are not asking for a favour. Every one of these organisations **already has a page on the site**,
built and maintained for free, with their photos, hours and directions. The ask is only that they
point at it — the same way they already link to their Facebook page.

## Tier 2 — the 22 claimed owners (easiest, best conversion)

These have already signed up. They control a website, and they have a page they are proud of. This
is the highest-yield group per email sent, and the least awkward ask.

**Angle:** "Add a link to your Lompoc Locals page from your site — it helps locals find you and it
helps your page rank." Give them the exact HTML to paste.

## The asset that does the asking for us

Rather than requesting links one at a time, ship a **"Featured on Lompoc Locals" badge** — a small
brand-coloured image with a snippet of HTML that links back to that business's page. Owners paste it
into their footer. It converts a repeated ask into a self-serve thing, and every install is a
relevant, local, keyword-rich inbound link.

This is the piece that scales; the emails above are how the first twenty get installed.

## Rules

- **Email-only** — no phone. See the outreach notes.
- **Nothing sends without the exact email being shown and approved first.**
- Never ask for a reciprocal link in exchange for placement on the site. Paid or traded links are
  against Google's guidelines and would put the whole domain at risk. We ask because the page is
  genuinely theirs and genuinely useful.
