# News & Events Backlink Playbook

Inbound links are earned, not generated. Every story we publish names real
organizations and businesses — each one is a link opportunity. Rule: **email
only**, one short message, one link, one ask. No sends without founder approval
of the exact email.

## The loop (per story)
1. Publish the story on /news (source credited by link — that's our side of the deal).
2. Email the subject/org: "we wrote about you — here's the link." Ask for one
   thing: a link from their site's news/press/partners page, or a share.
3. Log it below. Follow up once after 10 days. Never twice.

## Standing placements (one-time asks, permanent links)
- **Lompoc Valley Chamber of Commerce** — member listing + "community resources" link
- **Explore Lompoc (explorelompoc.com)** — local media / resources page
- **City of Lompoc** — community links page
- **Lompoc Public Library** — local resources
- **Allan Hancock College** community page (we cover the fire academy, football)
- **Wikipedia — Lompoc, California** external links: only once /news is clearly a real news source (6+ months, consistent cadence); premature = reverted

## Platform submissions (founder does once, 20 min total)
- **Google Search Console** → Links report: our current inbound baseline (check monthly)
- **Google News Publisher Center** → add lompoclocals.com/news (feeds: /api/blog/rss, /news-sitemap.xml)
- **Bing Webmaster Tools** → verify site, submit both sitemaps
- **Apple News / Flipboard** → optional, RSS-driven
