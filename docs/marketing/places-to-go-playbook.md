# Places to Go — the local attention engine

**The idea:** stop leading with deals. Lead with *where to go in Lompoc.* Deals are a reason
to open the site once. Places are a reason to follow the account forever — and the audience
that follows for places is the same audience a business wants to reach.

Explore Lompoc has spent years building the definitive list of what's worth seeing here.
We don't need to rediscover it. We need to **re-angle it.**

---

## 1. What Explore Lompoc is, and where the gap is

Explore Lompoc (explorelompoc.com) is the city's tourism marketing site — funded to fill hotel
rooms. Look at their blog and the frame is unmistakable:

> "Escape the Heat: A Coastal **Getaway**" · "7 Things to Do on Your Summer **Getaway**" ·
> "The Perfect Couples **Weekend**" · "**Vacation** with a View" · "Your Budget-Friendly
> **Getaway** Guide"

Every piece is written to someone who doesn't live here. That's their job. It's also the gap.

| | Explore Lompoc | Lompoc Locals |
|---|---|---|
| Written for | Someone planning a trip | Someone who lives here |
| Frame | "Come visit for a weekend" | "You drive past this every day" |
| Timescale | Itineraries, seasons | Tonight, Saturday, this week |
| Language | English | **English y español** |
| Business coverage | Curated tourism directory | **475 businesses, everyone included** |
| Bias | The photogenic stuff | The taquería, the tire shop, the barber |

**Nobody in Lompoc follows a tourism board for their own weekend plans.** That's the audience
we take — and it's bigger than the visitor audience, because it lives here year-round.

### What we take, and what we don't

**Take (facts — not ownable by anyone):** the list of places, addresses, trail lengths, park
names, which taquerías are on the taco trail, event dates. We already sync their event
calendar into `/events` (source `explorelompoc`), which is why we have 93 upcoming events.

**Don't take:** their photos, their written descriptions, their itinerary copy, their branded
program names. We shoot our own photos, write our own words, and name our own series.
Lifting their prose would be both wrong and pointless — their prose is written for tourists
and ours shouldn't sound like it.

## 3. Five series to run

Franchises, not one-offs. A named recurring series is what makes people follow instead of
just liking.

### ① "You've driven past it 500 times"
**The flagship.** One place per week that locals pass constantly and have never stopped at.

- Hook: *"You've driven past this 500 times. Have you ever actually stopped?"*
- Body: what it is, one thing most people don't know, exactly where to park, what it costs.
- ES: *"Has pasado por aquí 500 veces. ¿Alguna vez te has parado?"*
- First ten: Lompoc Museum · Cypress Gallery · the mural on H St · La Purísima's back trails ·
  Ryon Park · the Wine Ghetto · Miguelito Park · the Space & Missile Technology Center ·
  Beattie Park · Harris Grade at sunset.

**Why it works:** it's a mild accusation, and people answer accusations in the comments.
Half will say "I go all the time" and half will say "I've lived here 20 years and never went."
Both are engagement.

### ② "Free in Lompoc"
Every Friday, one thing that costs nothing. Beach, trail, mural walk, launch viewing,
museum free day, park, the river path.

- Hook: *"This weekend, for $0."*
- ES: *"Este fin de semana, por $0."*
- **This is the highest-shared format in a working-class town.** Don't dress it up.

### ③ "15 minutes from your house"
Places measured in drive time from the middle of town, not in "getaway" language.

- Hook: *"7 minutes from H Street. Most people here have never been."*
- Works especially well for Jalama (~50 min, and worth naming honestly), Surf Beach (~20),
  Harris Grade (~10), La Purísima (~8).
- **Be honest about the drive.** Overselling a distance is the one thing locals will call out.

### ④ The taco trail — locals' edition
Explore Lompoc's taco trail is built for visitors with a printed map. Ours is one taquería a
week, in Spanish first, from someone who actually eats there.

- Hook: *"Taco trail, week 3: [name]. Order the [thing]."*
- Post the ES caption **first** on this series and English second. This is the one series where
  the primary audience is Spanish-speaking Lompoc, and the account should show it.
- Tag them every time. Taquerías reshare, and most of them are already in our database.
- **This series is also the sales pipeline** — eleven businesses, one a week, each one getting
  free promotion before anyone asks them for anything.

### ⑤ "Where to take your out-of-town family"
Recurring, because everyone here hosts eventually and nobody has a plan.

- Hook: *"Your mom's visiting for the weekend. Here's the itinerary."*
- Three stops, one map, done. Rotate: the wine one, the beach one, the history one, the
  rainy-day one, the with-kids one.
- **This one quietly converts locals into tourism-board-grade advocates** — and it's the post
  most likely to get shared into a group chat, which is where local reach actually lives.

## 5. Cadence

Three posts a week is enough if they're all places.

| Day | Slot |
|---|---|
| **Tuesday** | ① "You've driven past it 500 times" — the flagship |
| **Thursday** | ④ Taco trail (ES first) or ⑤ family/visitor plan |
| **Friday** | ② "Free in Lompoc" — the weekend post |
| Any day | React to a launch, a closure, weather, or whatever the town is talking about |

Weekly events roundup and business spotlights continue on top of this — they're in
`docs/social-kit/content-weeks-2-3.md` and they don't conflict.

## 6. Making it convert

A place post with no destination is a tourism board post. Ours should always land somewhere:

1. **Build the page before the post.** A missing page turns a good post into a dead end.
2. **Every place page lists the businesses near it** — "3 minutes from Ryon Park:" then three
   real listings. That's the link between "places to go" and the businesses paying us.
3. **Every place post ends with the same soft line:** *"Every spot in Lompoc, one place —
   lompoclocals.com."* Not a pitch. A signature.
4. **The taco trail series doubles as outreach.** Post it, tag them, then email:
   *"We featured you this week — no charge, no catch. Your page is at [link] if you want it."*
   That's the warmest possible open, and it's already how `deal-ask-outreach.md` is framed.

**What to watch:** shares and saves, not likes. A place post that gets saved is someone
planning to go. That's the only metric on this that means anything.

## 7. The unfair advantage

Explore Lompoc can't do three things we can:

1. **Spanish.** Half this town, and their entire site is English-only.
2. **Everyone.** They list what a tourism board wants a visitor to see. We list the tire shop,
   the plumber, and the taquería on Laurel — 475 businesses, no curation gate.
3. **Today.** They publish itineraries. We can post "there's a launch tonight, look southwest"
   four hours before it happens.

Play those three and we're not competing with them — we're the thing they don't do.
